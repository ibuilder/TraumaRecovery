# Source notes

Working material for chapter revisions. Nothing in this directory is published
to the site; it is the audit trail between the author's original notes and the
prose in `client/src/lib/chapters/`.

- `journal-transcription.md` — transcription of the author's handwritten
  treatment-program journal (December; ACT, CBT, DBT, ACA and relapse-prevention
  groups). Uncertain readings are marked in square brackets.

The photographs themselves are not committed: they are personal medical
material, they are large, and the transcription is what the chapters are
written from.
