# Third-party material in the source notes

Four pages of the journal are **photographs of printed handouts from the
treatment programme**, not the author's own notes. They cannot be reproduced in
the book. Each is listed here with what can be used instead.

| IMG_0365 | "The Decision Table" | © 1999–2015 P. J. Carnes |
| IMG_0371 | "Eight Basic Emotions" | © Pia Mellody, Gentle Path at The Meadows |

**What we do instead.** The underlying models are published clinical frameworks
and are fine to teach, explain and build on — with attribution to a primary
source, in our own words, with our own examples and our own figures. What we do
not do is reproduce a handout's layout, its wording, or its table verbatim.

Primary sources to cite rather than the handouts:

- **Five core symptoms of codependence; the eight feelings; own vs carried
  feelings; the three ego states** — Pia Mellody, *Facing Codependence*
  (Harper & Row, 1989) and *Facing Love Addiction* (HarperOne, 1992).
- **The addiction cycle, the three circles, the sobriety challenges, the
  decision table, the stages of recovery, the new covenant** — Patrick Carnes,
  *Out of the Shadows* (3rd ed., Hazelden, 2001) and *Recovery Zone, Vol. 1*
  (Gentle Path Press, 2009).
- **The dopamine "rate" argument and addiction as a stress-induced,
  genetically mediated disorder of reward and executive function** —
  Kevin McCauley, *Pleasure Unwoven* (2010); cite the film and his talks
  rather than the chart.
- **Compulsive sexual behaviour disorder** — WHO, ICD-11 6C72, and
  Stefanie Carnes' published work.
